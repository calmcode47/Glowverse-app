# Privacy Policy

Glowverse values your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## Data We Collect
- Account details (e.g., email, name) for authentication
- Usage data to improve app performance and features
- Optional images for AR try-on and skin analysis (processed to deliver features)

## How We Use Data
- Provide core features like AR try-on, product recommendations, and order management
- Improve app performance, reliability, and user experience
- Send notifications about orders, promotions, and important updates (if enabled)

## Sharing
We do not sell personal data. We may share limited data with processors strictly for app functionality (e.g., image processing, analytics) under binding agreements.

## Security
We use industry-standard measures to safeguard your data. No method is 100% secure; we continuously improve our practices.

## Your Rights
You can access, update, or delete your account data within the app. Contact support for any additional requests.
